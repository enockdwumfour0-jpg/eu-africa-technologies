We build houses and other buildings. Our own block factory supplies blocks for our construction work, and every building in our programme is planned with solar panels, fibre internet, water and electricity.

Our building programme

For people who want to build in Ghana, you join a group and are placed in one of four divisions, depending on where you are starting from. Each division is planned to take one year to finish.

  • Pay a commitment fee of 30% of the total amount before we build
  • Pay a minimum monthly amount in dollars or euros into the contribution pot once building starts
  • Increase your monthly contribution whenever you like
  • Pay capital only, with no interest
  • Open to anyone with a passport

What is planned in every building

  • Solar panels
  • Fibre internet
  • Water
  • Electricity

Our gated community concept is planned with recreational facilities in secured areas.

Prices, and any service not listed on this page, are shared during the consultation or in a written proposal.

Four divisions

Where do you start?

Division 1

Division 2

You have your own land and your plans are ready.

Division 3

You want to live in a gated community.

Division 4

You want us to buy land anywhere in Ghana and build for you.
