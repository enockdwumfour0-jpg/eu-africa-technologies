Our Story

EU-AFRIC Technologies Ltd was founded in October 2026 in Accra, Ghana.

Our aim is to help people in the diaspora acquire land or build their dream real estate in Ghana. We want to make buying or building a house real, available and accessible, and to add value to the money the average person abroad spends on property in Ghana.

Two ways to get there

A loan through our financial institutions in Ghana. The applicant provides ID, pay slips, utility bills and a credit report from a national bank. When the loan is approved, the bank gives the funds to the real estate company building the house. The applicant then repays the loan with interest per annum in instalments, like a mortgage, after accepting to purchase the house.

Our social and affordable housing programme. Together with our real estate partners we created a concept to help the average working person. People who want to build in Ghana join a group with four divisions. Each division is planned to take one year to finish.

Growing with purpose

We created another business unit to generate funds that support our social programme. It is active in several sectors. We plan to build a solar-powered gated community and we have our own block factory.
