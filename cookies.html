Cookie Preferences

Effective date: 27 October 2026.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small files stored on your device when you visit a website. Similar technologies may also be used to remember preferences, understand website activity, and support website functionality.

2. How We May Use Cookies

Depending on the features enabled on our website, cookies may be used for the following purposes:

Essential Cookies

These support core website functions, security, and navigation. They cannot be switched off through this preference tool where they are strictly necessary for the website to operate.

Preference Cookies

These remember choices such as language or other website preferences, where available.

Analytics Cookies

These help us understand how visitors use the website so that we can improve its content and performance. They should only be activated where appropriate consent or another lawful basis is in place.

Marketing Cookies

These may be used to measure advertising effectiveness or support relevant marketing activities. They should only be activated where required consent has been obtained.

The categories listed above describe possible cookie uses. Actual cookies depend on the technologies installed on our website.

3. Managing Your Preferences

Where a cookie preference tool is provided, you may be able to:

  • Accept all cookies.
  • Reject non-essential cookies.
  • Choose which optional categories to allow.
  • Change or withdraw your preferences later.

Essential cookies may remain active where necessary for the website to function.

4. Browser Settings

You can also manage or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking certain cookies may affect some website features.

5. Third-Party Cookies

Some website features may rely on third-party services, such as embedded maps, videos, analytics, or external booking tools. Those services may use their own technologies and privacy policies.

Third-party features should be configured appropriately before they are enabled on the website.

6. Changes to This Notice

We may update this Cookie Preferences notice when our website technologies or legal requirements change.
