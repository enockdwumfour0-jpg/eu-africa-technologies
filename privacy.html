Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 October 2026.

1. Introduction

EU-AFRIC TECHNOLOGIES LTD (“we,” “our,” or “us”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you provide when using our website.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you visit our website, contact us, submit an enquiry, or express interest in our products and services.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information when you interact with our website:

  • Personal information: Your name, email address, telephone number, and country of residence.
  • Enquiry information: Details about your interest in land, construction, solar installations, building materials, or the Solar Village Project.
  • Communication records: Messages and other information you voluntarily provide when contacting us.
  • Technical information: Information such as browser type, device information, IP address, and website usage data, where collected through our website or service providers.

Please do not submit sensitive personal or financial information through general enquiry forms.

3. How We Use Your Information

We may use your information to:

  • Respond to enquiries and requests for information.
  • Provide details about our products, services, and projects.
  • Arrange consultations, site visits, or virtual meetings.
  • Prepare quotations and communicate about potential projects.
  • Improve our website and customer experience.
  • Maintain website security and prevent misuse.
  • Meet applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

We will use your information for the purposes communicated to you and where an appropriate lawful basis exists.

4. Marketing Communications

We may send promotional information where permitted by law and, where required, with your consent. You may ask us to stop sending marketing communications at any time.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties for their own marketing purposes.

5. Sharing Your Information

We may share relevant information with authorised employees, professional advisers, service providers, contractors, or business partners where necessary to respond to your enquiry or provide an agreed service.

We may also disclose information where required by law or necessary to protect our legal rights.

We will take reasonable steps to ensure that personal information is handled appropriately when shared.

6. International Data Transfers

Because we serve customers in Ghana and the diaspora, your information may be accessed or processed in more than one country where our service providers or business operations require it. Any such processing should be handled in accordance with applicable data protection requirements.

7. Data Security and Retention

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, or disclosure.

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, subject to applicable legal, contractual, and recordkeeping requirements.

No online system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Cookies and Website Technologies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to support essential website functions, remember preferences, understand website usage, or provide other features.

Where required, we will request your consent before using non-essential cookies. You can manage available cookie preferences through our Cookie Preferences page.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to request access to your personal information, request corrections, object to certain processing, or withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.

You may also raise concerns about how your information is handled.

10. Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to third-party websites or services. We are not responsible for their privacy practices. Please review their privacy policies before providing them with personal information.

11. Children's Privacy

Our website and services are intended for people seeking business, construction, property, or solar-related information. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information unnecessarily.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
