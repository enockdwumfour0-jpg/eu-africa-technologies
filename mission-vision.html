Mission & Vision

What we are here to do, and the sector we want to help build.

Mission

Make owning in Ghana possible

To help people in the diaspora acquire land or build their dream home in Ghana, and to make that goal real, available and accessible with a financial model that adds value to the money they invest.

Vision

A better name for Ghanaian real estate

To work with reputable companies and expand our services to the diaspora. Many people have lost money on real estate and been disappointed. We stand to stop these malpractices and create a positive image for the real estate sector in Ghana.

Our commitments

A clear financial model

A 30% commitment fee, then monthly contributions with no interest in our programme.

Reputable partners

We build with named partners and explain what each one does.

Open to the diaspora

Anyone with a passport can join our programme.
